# Brand Voice — ilken.github.io

A personal portfolio disguised as a music-streaming app. The conceit: _Ilken's career is a discography._

- **Tone**: playful but precise — music-app vocabulary applied to a engineer's CV ("Curated playlist", "Recently played", "Popular artists"). Never corporate.
- **Copy style**: short labels, sentence case, no exclamation marks. Section headers borrow streaming idioms; metadata lines look like track metadata (`Company · Role · 2023 – Present`).
- **Audience**: recruiters, collaborators, fellow engineers who'll smile at the bit and stay for the work.
- **The one memorable thing**: the player actually plays — 30-second previews of Linkin Park, maNga and Duman drive the whole page chrome.
